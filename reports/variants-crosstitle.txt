=== 2. the compressor's preamble, position by position
A column with one distinct value is a constant the compressor
writes; a column with 256 is random.  For the block codec the
window starts at +9, which is the first byte of the stream and
therefore the first control byte, not the header.

  XCompress streams, from +0: 0 payloads
  OFFSET DISTINCT  MOST COMMON VALUE

  codec block streams, from +9: 0 payloads
  OFFSET DISTINCT  MOST COMMON VALUE

  the 2003 GameCube and 2008 Wii figure, for comparison:
  +8..+11 are 5b 80 80 8d in 2,051 of 2,051 MSCF payloads, with
  the four bytes in front of them uniformly random.
